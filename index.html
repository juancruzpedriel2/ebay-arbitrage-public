Internal eBay Product Research and Purchasing Decision Support

This is a private internal application used to research products listed on eBay US for potential manual purchase and subsequent resale in Argentina.

The application uses eBay's Browse API to search configured product models, retrieve listing information, and maintain observations for product research. It calculates estimated landed cost, profit, and ROI using operator-configured economic assumptions.

All purchasing decisions and purchases are performed manually by the operator. The application does not automatically purchase items, place bids, submit offers, snipe auctions, create eBay listings, reprice listings, or fulfill eBay orders.

API usage is bounded by explicit call budgets, pagination limits, and result limits to minimize unnecessary traffic.
